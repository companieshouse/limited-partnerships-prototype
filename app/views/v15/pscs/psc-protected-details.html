PSCs can apply to protect their details if they are at serious risk of violence or intimidation because of the limited partnership's activities.

To apply for protection, they need to

  • submit a protection application and their PSC details by post
  • include evidence that they are eligible to have their details protected
  • pay a £100 fee

For more information, read the guidance on applying to protect your details.
