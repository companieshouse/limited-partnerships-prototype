The PSC needs to apply for protection before you can continue

The PSC needs to submit an application for protection and provide their PSC details using paper forms.

You should wait for them to do this before you continue, otherwise this registration may be rejected.

What you need to do next

Tell the limited partnership that the PSC needs to apply for protection (opens in new tab) before this registration is submitted.

In their protection application, they must provide the limited partnership name as: MORRIS LIMITED PARTNERSHIP. If the name in their application does not match this registration, we'll ask them to resubmit it.

When the PSC receives a 'confirmation of receipt' letter from us, you can submit this registration. You don't need to wait for the protection application to be accepted.

You should only submit this registration after Companies House has confirmed receipt of the PSC's protection application.
